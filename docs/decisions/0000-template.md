# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles
- Related: issues, PRs, other ADRs, audit sections

## Context

What situation forces a decision. Facts, constraints, and what happens if nothing is decided. Two to five sentences; link to evidence rather than restating it.

## Decision

What we will do, stated so that a reviewer can tell whether a PR complies.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder or is ruled out.
- What work this creates, and where it is tracked.

## Alternatives considered

One line each: the alternative and why it lost.
